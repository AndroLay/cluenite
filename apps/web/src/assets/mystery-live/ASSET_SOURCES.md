# Mystery Live artwork inventory

These PNG files were generated as prototype art for the local Mystery Live vertical slice and are imported by `MysteryLiveBoard.vue` and `mysteryLiveScene.ts`. They are not final commissioned assets. The briefs below record the visual direction used for generation; they are summaries, not verbatim prompt logs.

| File                 | Use                                            | Generation brief                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `museum-gallery.png` | Main Mystery Live PixiJS scene                 | A contemporary small-city museum gallery at night with an empty trophy display; cinematic illustrated environment, layered walnut/espresso surfaces, brass details and amber light, no lettering or blue palette. |
| `trophy-photo.png`   | Trophy photograph evidence and related records | An archival pre-incident photograph of a trophy inside a glass museum case; warm noir grade, legible photographic composition, no readable lettering.                                                             |
| `heat-warning.png`   | Display temperature warning                    | Close-up of an amber warning light beside a glass exhibit case; warm museum lighting and restrained paper/metal detail, no blue palette.                                                                          |
| `access-log.png`     | Access record and work order                   | Open access ledger with a keycard on a museum desk; tactile paper, dark wood, brass and amber light, no readable text.                                                                                            |
| `trolley-cctv.png`   | Trolley CCTV and cart-mark evidence            | A grainy museum CCTV still with a covered trolley in the gallery; night surveillance mood, warm sepia/brown treatment, no captions or lettering.                                                                  |

The generated assets are bundled in the web application and included in source archives. Final delivery still needs art direction review, evidence-specific crops/variants, and any required rights or attribution review before public release.
